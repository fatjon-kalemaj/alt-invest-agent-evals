# Solstice Home Services — Confidential Information Memorandum

## Overview
Solstice provides residential HVAC, plumbing and electrical repair and replacement services across the Phoenix and Tucson metro areas, with 260 technicians and a membership program of 41,000 households.

## Financial Summary
LTM revenue was $73.3 million, up 12% year over year, entirely organic. LTM EBITDA was $10.8 million. Membership renewal rates have been above 80% in each of the last three years. The company serves consumers directly; no customer is material.

## Management and Succession
Founder Robert Hale, 68, intends to retire at closing. CEO Lauren Kim joined three years ago from a national home-services platform and has run day-to-day operations since; the full leadership team has committed to remain post-transaction.

## Legal
A wage-and-hour class action filed in 2024 was settled in March for $450,000, fully covered by insurance, and the company has since adopted an electronic timekeeping system.
