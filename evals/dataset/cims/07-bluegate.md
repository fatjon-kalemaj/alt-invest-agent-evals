# Bluegate Payments — Confidential Information Memorandum

*Fictional company; synthetic document for evaluation purposes.*

## Overview
Bluegate is a payment facilitator providing card acceptance, invoicing and next-day funding to approximately 28,000 small businesses, primarily restaurants and home-service providers.

## Financial Summary
LTM revenue was $44.9 million, an increase of 27% over the prior year, and LTM EBITDA was $6.0 million. Revenue is presented gross of interchange and card network fees; net revenue after those pass-through costs was $15.2 million. Growth slowed to 11% in the first quarter, consistent with the seasonal pattern of the restaurant segment in each of the past four years, before re-accelerating in the second quarter.

## Customers
No merchant accounts for more than 1% of processing volume. The largest merchant represents 0.6% of revenue.

## Regulatory and Partners
Bluegate processes through a single sponsor bank. In April, the sponsor bank entered into a consent order with its federal regulator concerning oversight of third-party payment relationships, and has informed Bluegate that enhanced merchant-onboarding reviews will be required.

## Investment Highlights
- Embedded payments in under-served SMB verticals
- High-margin software attach (invoicing, payroll)
