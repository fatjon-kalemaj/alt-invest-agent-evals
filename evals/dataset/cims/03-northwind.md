# Northwind Fieldware — Confidential Information Memorandum

*Fictional company; synthetic document for evaluation purposes.*

## Overview
Northwind Fieldware sells vertical software for residential HVAC and plumbing contractors: scheduling, dispatch, quoting and invoicing. It serves approximately 3,400 contractor businesses in the United States.

## Financial Summary
LTM revenue was $31.5 million, a 41% increase year over year. LTM EBITDA was $4.1 million. Gross margin was 74%, down from 76% the prior year due to temporary duplicate hosting costs during the migration to a new cloud provider, which was completed in May; management expects gross margin to return to 76% this year.

Net revenue retention for the trailing twelve months was 88%, compared with 104% in the prior year, as smaller contractors downgraded or churned following the price increase in January.

## Customers
The customer base is highly fragmented. The largest customer represents 3.8% of revenue.

## Technology and IP
The core scheduling engine was written by co-founder and CTO Elena Sorensen. The underlying routing algorithm is patented in Ms. Sorensen's name and licensed to the company under a five-year license agreement that is terminable by either party on 90 days' notice.
