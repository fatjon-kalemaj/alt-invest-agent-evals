## Overview
Ridgeview operates 23 outpatient behavioral health clinics in Ohio offering therapy, psychiatric medication management and intensive outpatient programs for adolescents and adults.

## Financial Summary
LTM revenue was $67.4 million, an increase of 18% over the prior year driven by four new clinics and higher visit volume. Management estimates Adjusted EBITDA margin in the high teens; audited EBITDA figures for the period will be provided in the data room.

## Payer Mix
Ohio Medicaid, paid through its managed care plans, is the single largest payer at 61% of revenue. Commercial insurance represents 31% and self-pay the remainder. The Ohio Department of Medicaid has published a proposed 7% reduction in outpatient behavioral health reimbursement rates, subject to public comment, with a proposed effective date of next July.

## Workforce
Ridgeview employs 310 licensed clinicians. Clinician turnover was 19% last year, in line with industry benchmarks, and the company maintains a funded training program for newly licensed therapists.
