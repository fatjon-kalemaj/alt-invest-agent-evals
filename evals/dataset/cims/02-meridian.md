# Meridian Dental Partners — Confidential Information Memorandum

*Fictional company; synthetic document for evaluation purposes.*

## Overview
Meridian Dental Partners is a dental support organization (DSO) providing non-clinical services to 41 affiliated general dentistry practices in Texas and Oklahoma.

## Financial Summary
LTM revenue was $52.0 million and LTM EBITDA was $8.3 million. Total revenue grew 22% compared with the prior year, driven by the addition of nine practices. Same-store revenue at practices owned for more than twelve months declined 2% over the same period.

Capital expenditures increased to $4.6 million, reflecting de novo build-outs of three new offices. Management classifies this as growth capex; maintenance capex has averaged 1.5% of revenue over three years.

## Payers
Revenue is diversified across commercial dental plans, self-pay patients and Medicaid. No single payer is disclosed as material.

## Regulatory
In the second quarter, the Texas Health and Human Services Commission notified the company of a pending audit of Medicaid claims submitted by 12 affiliated practices for the period 2023–2025. The audit is ongoing and management has not estimated potential recoupment.

## Management
The management team has been in place since 2021 and is led by CEO Marcus Ortega, formerly COO of a national DSO.

- Proven acquisition engine: nine practices added in twelve months
- Fragmented market with a deep pipeline of founder-owned practices
