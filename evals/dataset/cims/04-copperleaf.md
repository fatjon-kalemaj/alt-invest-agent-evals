# Copperleaf Specialty Foods — Confidential Information Memorandum

*Fictional company; synthetic document for evaluation purposes.*

## Overview
Copperleaf makes premium refrigerated dips, spreads and salsas sold under the Copperleaf brand in more than 9,000 retail doors nationwide.

## Financial Summary
LTM revenue reached $118.0 million, up 6% versus the prior year. LTM EBITDA was $14.9 million. Avocado and dairy input costs are hedged through 12-month fixed-price contracts covering roughly 80% of expected volume, which kept gross margin within a one-point band over the past three years.

## Customers
Costco is the largest customer at 24% of revenue, under a relationship spanning 11 years. The remainder is spread across grocery, natural and club channels.

## Operations
Copperleaf does not own manufacturing. A single co-packer, Valley Fresh Co-Pack, produces approximately 70% of the company's volume under a supply agreement that renews annually. The remaining volume is split between two smaller co-packers.

## Legal
In 2025 the company conducted a voluntary recall of its Smoked Jalapeño Dip after a Listeria detection at the co-packer. Two personal-injury lawsuits related to the recall remain pending, and the company's insurer has reserved its rights regarding coverage.

## Investment Highlights
- Leading brand in a growing premium refrigerated category
- Asset-light model with high return on invested capital
