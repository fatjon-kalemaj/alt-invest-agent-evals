# Harborline Logistics, Inc. — Confidential Information Memorandum

*Prepared for prospective investors. Fictional company; synthetic document for evaluation purposes.*

## Overview
Harborline Logistics is a temperature-controlled third-party logistics (3PL) provider operating six cold-storage warehouses across New Jersey, Pennsylvania and Maryland. The company serves regional grocery chains, meal-kit companies and specialty food distributors.

## Financial Summary
For the last twelve months ended June 30, Harborline generated LTM revenue of $84.2 million, up 14% year over year, and Adjusted EBITDA of $11.6 million. Revenue dipped in the third quarter of the prior fiscal year during the relocation of the Edison facility; volumes fully recovered in the fourth quarter and the new site is operating at 91% utilization.

Adjusted EBITDA includes add-backs of $3.1 million for "non-recurring facility transition costs." Similar facility transition add-backs were also taken in each of the prior two fiscal years.

## Customers
Harborline has long-standing relationships with more than 60 customers. Its largest customer, a Mid-Atlantic grocery chain, accounted for 38% of LTM revenue. The master services agreement with this customer expires in March of next year and renewal discussions have not yet begun.

## Management
CEO Dana Whitfield founded the company in 2009. The company's CFO departed in January; Priya Raman, previously VP Finance at a public logistics company, was appointed CFO in February after a completed search and has led the last two quarterly closes.

## Investment Highlights
- Scarce cold-storage capacity in a supply-constrained corridor
- 14% growth with expanding customer count
- Opportunity to add value-added services (kitting, blast freezing)
