# Granite Peak Waste Services — Confidential Information Memorandum

*Fictional company; synthetic document for evaluation purposes.*

## 1. Company Overview
Granite Peak provides residential and commercial solid waste collection, transfer and disposal services in Colorado and Wyoming. The company operates 212 collection vehicles, three transfer stations and one owned municipal solid waste landfill with an estimated remaining life of 31 years.

## 2. Financial Summary
LTM revenue of $142.5 million is presented pro forma for the acquisition of Ridge Hauling. Ridge Hauling's acquisition is expected to close in the first quarter, subject to state regulatory approval. Excluding Ridge Hauling, LTM revenue was $118.3 million and LTM EBITDA was $25.9 million, compared with pro forma EBITDA of $31.2 million. Revenue excluding Ridge Hauling grew 8% year over year.

|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 118.3 | 24.2 | 142.5 |
| EBITDA | 25.9 | 5.3 | 31.2 |

Fuel surcharge clauses in customer contracts recover approximately 90% of diesel cost changes within one quarter.

## 3. Capital Structure
Net debt at period end was $84 million, or 2.7x pro forma EBITDA. Asset retirement obligations for landfill closure and post-closure care total approximately $28 million on an undiscounted basis. These obligations are not included in the net debt figure presented in this memorandum.

## 4. Fleet
Following a three-year replacement program completed this year, the average age of the collection fleet is 4.1 years. 38% of trucks run on compressed natural gas.

## 5. Customers and Contracts
Granite Peak serves approximately 151,000 residential and 9,800 commercial customers. The company's largest contract, residential collection for the City of Larkspur, generated $19.4 million in LTM revenue. The contract term ends next June, and the city has announced it will issue a competitive request for proposals in the spring.

## 6. Employees
The company employs 640 people. Driver turnover was 24%, below the industry average.

## 7. Growth Strategy
- Close and integrate Ridge Hauling
- Increase landfill internalization from 58% to 70% of collected volume
- Pursue tuck-in acquisitions of independent haulers
