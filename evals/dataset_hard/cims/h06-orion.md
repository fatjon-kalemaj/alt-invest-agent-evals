## 1. Company Overview
Orion is a managed service provider (MSP) delivering outsourced IT support, cybersecurity monitoring and cloud management to 1,050 small and mid-sized businesses in the Carolinas. Orion's revenue is 94% recurring.

| ($ millions) | Prior year | LTM |

| Gross profit | 17.1 | 19.4 |

Revenue grew 11% year over year. Revenue churn rose to 6% in the second quarter as Orion exited 140 low-margin break-fix customers by design; excluding these planned exits, churn was 0.8%, in line with the trailing average.

## 4. Leadership
Co-founder Mark Ellis stepped down as CEO in January; COO Sara Lund, who has run operations since 2019, was appointed CEO under a succession plan approved by the board in 2023. The rest of the leadership team is unchanged.

## 5. Security
In 2024 a client experienced a ransomware incident originating from a compromised third-party tool. A forensic review found no Orion liability, the client's losses were covered by its cyber insurance, and the tool was removed from Orion's stack.

## 6. Employees
Orion employs 186 people, 140 of them technical staff. 92% of technicians hold at least one vendor certification.
