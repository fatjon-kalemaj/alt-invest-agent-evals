# Pinnacle Clinical Staffing — Confidential Information Memorandum

*Fictional company; synthetic document for evaluation purposes.*

## 1. Company Overview
Pinnacle places registered nurses, allied health professionals and travel clinicians with hospitals and health systems in 19 states. The company maintains a network of roughly 6,800 credentialed clinicians and filled 9,200 assignments in the last twelve months.

## 2. Financial Summary

| ($ millions) | Prior year | LTM |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 120.0 | 157.2 |
| Gross profit | 30.6 | 38.9 |
| EBITDA | 11.5 | 15.8 |

Revenue grew 31% over the prior year. Gross margin declined one point in the first half as clinician pay increases took effect before bill-rate escalators; the escalators took effect in July and second-half gross margin returned to the prior-year level.

Free cash flow was negative in the first quarter, as in every year, because the company prepays its annual malpractice insurance premium in January.

## 3. Balance Sheet Highlights
Accounts receivable were $31.9 million at period end, compared with $18.4 million a year earlier. The company has no term debt; its asset-based revolver had $6.0 million drawn at period end.

## 4. Clients
Pinnacle works with 212 client facilities. The largest client, a regional health system, represents 5.4% of revenue. Average client tenure is 5.8 years.

## 5. Pricing
Bill rates are negotiated per assignment under master services agreements. Approximately 38% of LTM revenue was billed under crisis-rate contracts with eleven health systems; these contracts expire within the next six months, and the company expects renewal bill rates 25% to 30% lower.

## 6. Clinician Workforce
Clinicians are recruited through a proprietary mobile app, which generated 61% of new applicants last year. Approximately 22% of clinicians are engaged as independent contractors. In August, the company received an information request from the state Department of Labor regarding worker classification, which management characterizes as routine.

## 7. Technology
The credentialing platform reduces time-to-placement to an average of nine days, compared with an industry average of fourteen.

## 8. Growth Strategy
- Expand into allied health (respiratory therapy, imaging)
- Add managed service provider (MSP) programs with large health systems
- Grow the per-diem local staffing business
