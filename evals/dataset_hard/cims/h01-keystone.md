# Keystone Industrial Distribution — Confidential Information Memorandum

*Fictional company; synthetic document for evaluation purposes.*

## 1. Company Overview
Keystone Industrial Distribution supplies maintenance, repair and operations (MRO) products — fasteners, power transmission components, safety equipment and cutting tools — to manufacturers and contractors across Pennsylvania, Ohio and West Virginia. Founded in 1987 by Tom Brandt, who remains CEO, Keystone operates three distribution centers and 14 branch counters and carries roughly 62,000 SKUs. Next-day fill rate was 97.4% over the last twelve months.

## 2. Market
The regional MRO distribution market is fragmented: the top three national distributors hold less than a third of the market in Keystone's territory, and the long tail consists of family-owned branches. Customers value same-day availability and technical support from counter staff over price, which has historically protected gross margin.

## 4. Working Capital and Liquidity
Inventory turns were 4.6x, in line with the prior two years. The company draws on its revolver each spring to build inventory ahead of the construction season and has repaid the balance in full by the fourth quarter in each of the last five years.

## 6. Facilities
The company leases its three distribution centers from Keystone Realty LLC, an entity owned by CEO Tom Brandt. Rent of $0.6 million per year is below market; an independent appraisal commissioned by the company estimated market rent at $2.1 million per year. The leases are month-to-month.

## 7. Employees
Keystone employs 286 people, including 74 counter and inside-sales staff. Voluntary turnover was 11% last year.
