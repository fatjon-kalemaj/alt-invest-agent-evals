# Clearwater Specialty Pharmacy — Confidential Information Memorandum

*Fictional company; synthetic document for evaluation purposes.*

## 1. Company Overview
Clearwater is an independent specialty pharmacy dispensing high-cost medications for rheumatology, dermatology, oncology and rare diseases. It operates from a single URAC- and ACHC-accredited facility in Tampa and ships to patients in 46 states.

## 2. Financial Summary

| ($ millions) | Prior year | LTM |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 178.2 | 212.0 |
| Gross profit | 21.4 | 27.6 |
| EBITDA | 10.3 | 16.1 |

Revenue grew 19% year over year. Gross profit includes $4.2 million of DIR fee reconciliation credits received this year relating to claims from prior years. Prescription volume fell 6% in March during a system migration; volumes recovered by May and the migration is complete.

## 3. Therapeutic Mix
Dispensing of Veltrimab, a biologic for rheumatoid arthritis, accounted for 44% of revenue. Dermatology accounts for 21% and oncology for 17%.

## 4. Market
Specialty drugs are the fastest-growing segment of pharmacy spending. The first biosimilar to Veltrimab is expected to launch in the second half of next year. Payers have generally moved patients to biosimilars within 12 to 18 months of launch.

## 5. Payers and Access
The largest pharmacy benefit manager accounts for 29% of dispensing revenue. The company holds limited-distribution access for six specialty drugs; all six agreements were renewed this year for three-year terms.

## 6. Licensing and Ownership
The pharmacy permit and the company's payer network credentials are held in the name of founder and pharmacist-in-charge Anita Desai. A change of ownership requires approval of the state board of pharmacy and re-credentialing with payers, which management estimates will take 60 to 120 days.

## 7. Operations
Clearwater employs 118 people, including 22 pharmacists. Average time from referral to first fill is 3.1 days.
