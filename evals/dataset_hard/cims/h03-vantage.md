# Vantage Learning Systems — Confidential Information Memorandum

*Fictional company; synthetic document for evaluation purposes.*

## 1. Company Overview
Vantage sells a cloud platform for K-12 school districts covering formative assessment, intervention tracking and progress reporting. The platform is used by 1,140 districts in 38 states, reaching approximately 3.2 million students. Contracts are typically one to three years, invoiced annually in advance at the start of the school year.

## 4. Bookings and Deferred Revenue
Deferred revenue was $17.5 million at June 30, compared with $22.0 million a year earlier. Approximately 34% of current-year bookings were funded by districts from federal ESSER relief funds, which are not available for new obligations after September 30 of this year.

## 5. Customers
The largest district customer represents 2.1% of revenue. Logo churn rose to 9% in the fourth quarter as the company retired its legacy gradebook product as planned; churn excluding the retired product was 3%, consistent with prior years.

## 6. Security and Compliance
The prior-year SOC 2 Type II report included one exception related to access reviews; it was remediated and the current-year report contains no exceptions. Vantage is a signatory to the Student Privacy Pledge.

## 7. Team
The company has 240 employees, 96 of them in product and engineering.
